How to Play

Move: WASD / Arrow keys / mobile joystick

Attack: Space, J, mouse click, or ⚔️ button

Interact: E / 💬 button — talk to villagers, pick up shards

Drink potion: H / 🧪 button   Mega potion: 💊 button   Quest log: Q   Inventory: I   World map: M   Pause: Esc / P

Collect the 3 elemental shards from the Forest, Desert and Snowfields, then face Malachar in the Shadowlands. Talk to everyone in Ember Village — the shop, side quests and the main quest all start there. Beyond the village lie the Sunset Coast, the Stormpeak Highlands, and the new Moonlit Vale to the far east (Moonhaven, Moonlit Lake, Vale Ruins, Starfall Observatory), with new NPCs, quests, treasure chests, and tougher enemies.

Adult Story Mode (optional, 21+ only): off by default. Turn it on from the title or pause menu — you must enter a birth year showing you are 21 or older and confirm “I am 21 or older”. No birth year is stored. It adds optional adult romance (Elara, 27, and Kael, 29) and a mature tavern quest; the main story works the same with it on or off.

The Ember Falls

Long ago, the Ember Crystal kept the seven lands in balance. Then Malachar, the Ashen King, shattered it and fled to the Shadowlands.

Three shards survived — hidden in the Whispering Forest, the Sunscorch Desert and the Frostbite Snowfields, each watched by a guardian.

You are the last Emberbearer. Elder Mara is waiting for you in Ember Village.

🔞 Adult Story Mode — 21+ Only

Content warning: mature themes — romance, alcohol in the tavern, violence, and moral choices. Intimate moments are suggestive dialogue with a fade-to-black only, never graphic detail.

Adult Story Mode is off by default. Romance here is adult-to-adult and consensual only, and you can decline or end a romance at any time. There are no child characters in this game.

Only a yes/no flag is saved. Your birth year is never stored.

💀 You Have Fallen

The Ember flickers… but does not go out. You wake in Ember Village, 10% of your gold lost.

🌟 The Ember Restored

Malachar crumbles to ash. The three shards rise from your pack, fuse in a column of warm light, and the Ember Crystal blazes whole once more.

Spring returns to the Snowfields. The desert blooms at its edges. And in Ember Village, they light the lanterns in your honour.
